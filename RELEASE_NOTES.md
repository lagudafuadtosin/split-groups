Two new layouts under Layout in the view options. Cards: a grid of cards in every group, with an image at the top when an Image property is set. Kanban: one column per group, side by side, and a card can be dragged to another column, which changes that note's list property the way "Dropping a card on a group" says (move, add, or replace). Only the split property is changed, and only on a drop. Then split by is not used in Kanban. Needs Obsidian 1.10.2 or later.
